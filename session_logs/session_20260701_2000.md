### 3. Instrument Family Reconciliation — 4-Family vs 9-Family (P1 — COMPLETE, answers H3)

Source: `csv/acer_revenue_model_20260630.csv`

Verified the 4-family grouping (script matrix, Session 39) is an exact superset grouping of
the 9-family `Instrument_Family` (revenue model, Session 38) — record counts match precisely
(10,093 = 10,093, no records lost or double-counted).

**Key finding:** the collapse hides real fee variance in 2 of 4 buckets. "Fund-Based/Other"
folds in Fund-Based Facilities (₹5.00L, 803 records, 29.8% of the bucket) with Letter of
Credit/Non-Fund-Based/Other (₹3.00L, 70.2%) — a 67% fee spread. "Term Loan" folds in ST Bank
Facilities (₹2.50L, 228 records, 4.9%) with Term Loan/LT Bank Facilities (₹4.97L, 95.1%) — a
2x spread but small in volume.

**No existing output is affected** — the revenue model already uses the correct 9-family fee
tiers throughout; the 4-family scheme was only ever used for pitch scripts, never for $
estimation. Recommendation: keep the 4-family grouping for call scripts (right granularity
for a sales conversation) and the 9-family grouping for all revenue modeling; if a future
script matrix session wants finer agency × instrument INC-rate differentiation, break out
Fund-Based Facilities as its own row (material volume + fee gap) but ST Bank Facilities can
stay folded into Term Loan (small volume).

Output: `instrument_family_reconciliation_20260701.md` (documentation deliverable, no CSV
per Rule 5 — not a company lead list)
