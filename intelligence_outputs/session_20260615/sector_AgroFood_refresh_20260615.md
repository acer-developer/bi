## Why This Sector, Why Now

**Timing is everything in Agro/Food.** June–July is the pre-Kharif cropping season — the period when:
- Agri-input companies (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides) are drawing on working capital limits
- Food processing units are ramping up procurement
- Rural NBFCs and cooperative lenders are disbursing seasonal credit
- Banks are reviewing crop loan books before the Kharif disbursal cycle

Companies that need ratings for working capital limits and bank guarantees need them **now**, before the July–August credit cycle peaks. A rating that lapses in July means missed credit lines.

**ACER's pitch window: June 15 – July 31, 2026.**
