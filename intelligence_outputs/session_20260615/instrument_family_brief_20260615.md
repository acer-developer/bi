## Purpose

Every company that approaches ACER for a rating has a specific instrument to be rated. Understanding which instrument families carry the highest INC exposure tells ACER's sales team:
1. **WHAT to pitch** — which instruments to offer first
2. **WHERE the distress is** — which instrument types are most abandoned by incumbent raters
3. **HOW to price** — which instrument families are most volume-intensive vs. complex

## Finding 1 — Short-Term Instruments Are the Biggest INC Problem

**Short-Term / Fund-Based Limits: 40.0% INC rate — highest INC concentration**

Short-term instruments include:
- Cash Credit (CC) limits
- Overdraft (OD) facilities
- Working Capital Demand Loans
- Packing Credit (export finance)
- Letter of Credit (LC)
- Bank Guarantee (BG)

These are the instruments banks use to extend **day-to-day working capital** to companies. When a rating lapses or goes INC, the bank **cannot renew** these limits at their annual review. This creates an immediate, tangible financial emergency.

**Why this matters for ACER:**
- 9,980 short-term instrument rows are INC = 9,980 instruments where the current rater has failed the company
- These companies are actively in trouble — they need a replacement rater now, not eventually
- ACER's pitch for short-term instruments: *"We'll restore your CC/OD limit renewal capability within 15 days"*

---

## Finding 2 — Long-Term Loans Are Almost As Bad (35.4% INC)

Long-term instruments include Term Loans, Project Finance, Equipment Finance, Capex Loans.

- 8,947 long-term instrument rows are INC
- 4,122 are HIGH urgency (overdue >12 months)
- Long-term ratings typically cover 3–5 years of a loan's life — if INC, the entire loan monitoring relationship is broken

**Why this matters:**
- Banks require annual ratings surveillance on long-term loans
- INC on a term loan means the bank's NPA classification risk increases
- Companies are motivated to fix this — not for themselves, but because their bank is pushing them

**ACER's pitch for long-term instruments:** *"Your bank's RBI compliance requires annual rating surveillance. Your current rater has gone silent. We'll step in, review the current position, and restore the monitoring relationship."*

## Finding 3 — Bank Guarantee & Letter of Credit Are the Hottest Instruments

Drilling into specific instruments, the top INC rates by instrument type:

| Instrument | INC Rate | INC Volume | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Short Term Facility (Infomerics) | **81.8%** | 9 | Small volume but extreme rate |
| Long Term Fund Based Facility | **64.3%** | 9 | Infomerics clients abandoning fast |
| Long/Short Term Bank Facilities | **53.3%** | 16 | Mixed facilities — high INC |
| Letter of Credit | **50.6%** | 2,543 | Massive volume + high INC |
| Long/Short Term Blended | **51.7%** | 30 | Multi-facility = multi-problem |
| Bank Guarantee | **48.6%** | 3,593 | Largest absolute INC volume |
| Term Loans | **37.2%** | 5,814 | Highest absolute INC count |

**Bank Guarantee (BG)** is the single most important target instrument:
- 3,593 INC BG instrument rows = 3,593 instances of a company needing a BG rating but having no valid rater
- BG ratings are required for public sector contracts, tenders, government procurement
- Companies with lapsed BG ratings **lose the ability to bid on government contracts immediately**
- Note: Team must confirm ACER offers BG ratings (flagged in Q3, session 8). If yes, this is an immediate unlock.

**Letter of Credit (LC)** is the second highest priority:
- 2,543 INC LC instrument rows
- LC ratings are required for import letters of credit — without a valid rating, banks block imports
- High prevalence in import-dependent sectors: Textiles (fabric/yarn import), Agro/Food (fertilizer import), Chemicals

## Finding 4 — Capital Market Instruments Have Lower but Significant INC (21.0%)

Capital Market / Structured Finance includes:
- Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs)
- Commercial Paper (CP)
- Bonds
- Asset-Backed Securities (ABS)
- Mortgage-Backed Securities (MBS)

- 4,268 unique D365 companies have capital market instrument ratings
- 1,700 INC instrument rows = 21.0% INC rate
- These are typically larger companies (NCD issuers tend to be ₹100 Cr+)
- ACER's entry into capital markets is longer-term — but 618 HIGH urgency capital market instruments represent a real pipeline

**Strategic note:** ACER should not lead capital market outreach in Year 1. Focus on bank facility ratings (Short-term + Long-term). Capital markets require SEBI recognition as a registered credit rating agency — confirm this is in place before pursuing NCDs.

## Sales Team Briefing Points

**When a prospect asks "what do you rate?"**

Lead with: *"Bank guarantees, letters of credit, working capital limits, and term loans — everything your bank needs to maintain your credit facilities."*

Never lead with: NCDs, bonds, ABS — these are seen as complex/capital market and will trigger "are you SEBI certified?" questions.

**When a prospect says "we already have a rating"**

Ask: *"Is your rating current? Was it done in the last 12 months? Did your rating agency confirm renewal, or is it showing INC status?"*
— 40% of Short-term instrument ratings in the market are INC or overdue. This question will reveal opportunity in 4 out of 10 calls.
