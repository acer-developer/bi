## Key Finding: ACUITE April 2026 = 128 INC Companies (57.4% INC rate)

ACUITE's INC acceleration MIRRORS CARE's April 2026 spike. Both agencies hit their 2026 peaks in April:
- CARE April 2026: 233 companies INC
- ACUITE April 2026: 128 companies INC (57.4% INC rate — highest month in dataset)

This is NOT coincidental. April 2026 is the post-March fiscal year-end review cycle. Companies whose ratings came up for annual renewal in March/April were overwhelmingly marked non-cooperative.
