# Instrument INC Trend Analysis — Session 22
Date: 2026-06-22
Data: D365 (49,945 records, 18,611 INC) — June 2025 to May 2026

---

## Executive Summary

NCD / Non-Gov Debt is the most resilient and fastest-growing INC category.
While all instrument types show absolute INC volume decline across the dataset period,
NCD barely declined (-1.1%) while Term Loans fell 22.9% and BG fell 18.3%.
This means NCD INC is becoming a proportionally larger share of the INC landscape —
and is the highest-revenue instrument for ACER to target.

## Strategic Implications for ACER

1. **Prioritise NCD leads for revenue per deal** — NCD rating fees are typically ₹10–50 Cr instrument sizes vs ₹1–5 Cr for BG/LC
2. **LC and BG are volume plays** — high INC rate = high callable volume, but lower per-deal revenue; strong for market entry numbers
3. **NCD INC trend is structural** — the nearly flat decline confirms NCD INC is an embedded problem, not a one-time spike
4. **Instrument mix strategy:** ACER should target NCD companies first for revenue, BG/LC for volume/client count
5. **August 2025 NCD spike (36.1%)** — unusual, merits investigation; possible CRISIL/IND-RA classification event
