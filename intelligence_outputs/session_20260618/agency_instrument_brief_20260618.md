## Headline Findings

### Finding 1: BRICKWORK Has Catastrophically Abandoned Working Capital

BRICKWORK's INC rate exceeds **86%** for every working capital instrument:
- Bank Guarantee: 86.5% (910 INC out of 1,052)
- Letter of Credit: 87.9% (706 INC out of 803)
- Term Loans: 86.1% (1,148 INC out of 1,334)
- Fund-based: 71.9% (417 INC out of 580)
- Non-fund-based: 76.6% (210 INC out of 274)

**Total BRICKWORK working capital INC exposure: ~3,600 companies across these instruments**

This is systemic abandonment, not selective non-renewal. Any BRICKWORK client with a working capital instrument is a near-certain ACER prospect.

**Exception: Debentures/Bonds** — BRICKWORK's debenture INC rate is only 11.6% (vs 86%+ for working capital). BRICKWORK is maintaining their structured finance clients while abandoning their working capital base. This confirms BRICKWORK has strategically shifted away from SME working capital toward larger bond issuances.

### Finding 2: ICRA and IND-RA Are the Most Stable Raters for Working Capital

| Agency | BG INC | LC INC | Notes |
|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| ICRA | **14.9%** | **12.2%** | Lowest of all agencies for trade instruments |
| IND-RA | **10.0%** | 20.0% | Second-lowest for BG |

**Implication for ACER sales:** ICRA and IND-RA clients are the hardest to displace. They are satisfied with their current rater. Do not prioritize pitching to ICRA or IND-RA-rated BG/LC companies — the effort-to-conversion ratio is poor.

**Prioritize instead:** BRICKWORK, ACUITE, CRISIL, and CARE clients in this order.

### Finding 3: ACUITE Has High INC on Trade Instruments

ACUITE — often considered the "emerging challenger" — has surprisingly high INC rates:
- Letter of Credit: 52.2% (360 INC out of 690)
- Fund-based: 50.0% (36 INC out of 72)
- Term Loans: 43.6% (609 INC out of 1,396)
- Bank Guarantee: 43.4% (457 INC out of 1,054)

ACUITE is a direct competitor to ACER, but their INC rate is comparable to CRISIL and CARE. ACUITE clients are open to switching — they are not "safe" from ACER's pitch.

### Finding 4: Debentures/Bonds — Everyone's Best-Behaved Instrument

ALL agencies show near-zero INC on debentures/bonds:
- CRISIL: 0.8% | CARE: 0.7% | ICRA: 2.7% | IND-RA: 0.0% | ACUITE: 0.0%

Debentures and bonds are publicly listed instruments with SEBI-mandated periodic surveillance. Companies cannot go INC on these without triggering default events. This instrument category is effectively locked for displacement pitches.

**ACER should NOT target companies whose primary instrument is debentures/bonds** — the agency relationship is compliance-mandated and will not switch.

### Finding 5: CRISIL Non-Fund-Based Has Low INC (9.2%)

CRISIL's non-fund-based facility INC rate (9.2%) is far below their other instruments (BG: 44.5%, LC: 42.7%). Non-fund-based facilities are complex combined BG/LC limits — CRISIL appears to selectively maintain these relationships even where other instruments lapse. CRISIL non-fund-based clients are harder to displace than their BG/LC clients.
