## Why Steel & Metals Matters Now

India's steel sector is in a structural cyclical peak. Infrastructure spending (roads, railways, housing) and manufacturing capex (automobiles, capital goods) are driving steel demand through FY27. Companies in this sector are actively borrowing — they need bank guarantees for project contracts and letters of credit for raw material imports (coking coal, iron ore, scrap).

**58.8% INC rate** — more than half of rated steel companies have been abandoned. This is higher than the 45% overall market average, indicating the sector is underserved relative to its credit activity.
