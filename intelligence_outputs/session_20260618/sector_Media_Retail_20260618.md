## Sub-Sector Analysis

### Media Sub-Sector
Companies: Film studios, broadcasters, publications, digital media, advertising agencies
- **Film/Entertainment:** Short-term loans and term loans dominate (production financing)
- **Publications:** Working capital facilities for newsprint and distribution
- **Broadcast/Cable:** Long-term infrastructure loans; more stable, lower INC
- **Advertising agencies:** Working capital heavy; vulnerable to client payment cycles

### Retail Sub-Sector
Companies: Fashion retailers, mall developers, supermarket chains, e-commerce, QSR
- **Fashion retail:** BG for vendor advances + LC for import of fabric/apparel; INC often tied to inventory buildup post-COVID
- **Mall developers:** Term loans and long-term loans for capex; Construction/Infra overlap
- **E-commerce/QSR:** Newer companies; higher Infomerics presence (SME/MSME focus)

---

## Instrument Breakdown (D365)

Term loans dominate, followed by BG and LC for retail operations and media production. Retail chains use LC for goods imports; media houses use BG for broadcast equipment leasing and licensing commitments.
