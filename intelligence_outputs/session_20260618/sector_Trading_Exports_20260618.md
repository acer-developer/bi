## Why This Sector Matters Right Now

**Timing:** India's export season peaks July–September. Agri-commodity exporters (rice, cotton, spices) need fresh bank guarantees and letters of credit for export financing by July. Manufactured-goods exporters (engineering goods, textiles, gems) ramp shipments ahead of global retail season. Companies with lapsed ratings **cannot draw LC/BG facilities** — ACER's pitch is literal: "Without a fresh rating, your export line is frozen."

**Sector INC rate context:** Trading/Exports has 64.1% INC — the second-highest of all 18 sectors analyzed (after Paper/Packaging at 76.6%). Nearly two-thirds of all rated trading companies have been abandoned by their rater. This is a structurally open market.
