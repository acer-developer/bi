## Seasonal Opportunity

Mining sector ratings demand has a seasonal pattern tied to:
- **Monsoon prep (June–July):** Companies with quarry leases need ratings renewed before monsoon shutdown
- **Government tender season (Oct–Dec):** BG ratings needed for infrastructure project bids
- **Environmental compliance renewals (annual):** Mining licenses require valid credit ratings in several states

**Current timing:** June is peak for pre-monsoon operational decisions. The 21 ULTRA HOT companies need immediate outreach.
