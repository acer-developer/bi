## The Defining Insight: This Sector Has Been Abandoned

**76.6% INC rate** is the single highest INC rate across all 18 sectors ACER has analyzed. Three out of four paper/packaging companies have been abandoned by their current rating agency.

What makes this extraordinary: the INC rate is consistent across ALL urgency tiers:
- HIGH urgency: 71.4% INC
- MEDIUM urgency: **78.1% INC**
- LOW urgency: 76.0% INC

This is not a cohort of old ratings gone stale — the abandonment is sector-wide, structural, and across all time horizons. Paper and packaging companies are systematically underserved by the rating ecosystem.

**Letter of Credit INC rate: 95.0%** (19 of 20 LC-rated paper companies are INC). Every paper company with an LC facility has been abandoned by their rater. This is the highest single instrument × sector INC rate we have recorded.
