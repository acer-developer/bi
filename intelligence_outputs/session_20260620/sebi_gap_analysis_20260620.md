# SEBI License Gap Analysis — 2026-06-20
**Session 18 | D365: 49,945 records | Total INC analyzed: 18,610**

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis maps ACER's addressable market by instrument license category.
Credit rating agencies in India operate under two distinct regulatory buckets:
- **Bank Credit Ratings** — No SEBI registration needed (RBI governed)
- **Debt Securities / Capital Market Instruments** — SEBI CRA registration required

This determines which INC companies ACER can target TODAY vs which require
a SEBI capital market license first.

---

## The Two Markets

### Market 1: Bank Credit Ratings (Accessible TODAY)
No additional SEBI license needed. Includes:
- Term Loans
- Long Term Loans / Short Term Loans
- Bank Guarantees (BG)
- Letters of Credit (LC)
- Fund-based financial facilities
- Non-fund-based financial facilities
- Non-fund based working capital limits

### Market 2: Debt Securities / Capital Market (Requires SEBI License)
SEBI CRA (Credit Rating Agency) registration for these instruments:
- Non-government Debt (NCDs, Commercial Paper)
- Debentures / Bonds / Notes / Bills
