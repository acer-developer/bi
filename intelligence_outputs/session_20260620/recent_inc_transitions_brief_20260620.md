## What Are Recent INC Transitions?

A company "transitions to INC" when:
1. They previously had a normal (non-INC) rating at an agency
2. Their most recent rating at that same agency is INC
3. The INC transition happened after January 1, 2026

These are the **most recently frustrated companies** — their pain is fresh, their motivation to
switch is highest, and their emotional urgency is at peak.
