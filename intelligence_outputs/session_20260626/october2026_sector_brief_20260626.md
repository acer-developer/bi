## Key Intelligence

### Finding 1: Construction is ACER's #1 October Target Sector
344 companies, ₹53,422 Cr identified amount, 518 ULTRA HOT+HOT escalations. Construction
is present across ALL 7 agencies. ACER's pitch: faster turnaround, no SEBI restrictions
(BG and Term Loans don't require CRA license).

### Finding 2: Energy has the Highest Ticket Size
108 companies but ₹12,967 Cr — average ₹120 Cr per record. Infrastructure and Energy
are ticket-size leaders. Even 5-10 wins here = material revenue.

### Finding 3: Automobiles Cluster is Unusual
191 companies in Automobiles, 152 ULTRA HOT. This concentration suggests a credit
cycle event in Oct 2025 when auto sector INC ratings spiked. Possible: post-COVID
recovery stall + supply chain impact. CRISIL (95 companies) and CARE (34) dominate.

### Finding 4: Logistics has Highest ULTRA HOT Rate
66 of 72 Logistics companies (91.7%) escalate ULTRA HOT. Consistent with historical
finding: logistics is dominated by Brickwork INC with non-clearing pattern.

### Finding 5: CRISIL is the Single Largest Agency Pool
CRISIL alone accounts for 1,220 companies (38.4% of unique companies). Any ACER
sector focus should prioritize CRISIL displacement.

### Finding 6: Infomerics contributes 447 records
These are companies INC at Infomerics for 12+ months escalating Oct–Nov. Since
D365 has ZERO Infomerics records, these are ACER-exclusive leads with no competitor
overlap. Lower ticket size but zero competition.
