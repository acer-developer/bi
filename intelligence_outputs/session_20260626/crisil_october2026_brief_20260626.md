## Key Intelligence

1. **Construction dominates CRISIL October** — 124 companies, ₹8,428 Cr total. CRISIL is
   strongest in construction/infrastructure. ACER's pitch: "We rate construction companies
   faster; CRISIL backlog averages 6-8 weeks."

2. **953 ULTRA HOT escalations (42.3%)** — CRISIL has the single largest ULTRA HOT pool
   in October. These are currently MEDIUM urgency and will hit the 12-month mark in Oct.
   Start calling September 15 for a head start.

3. **Automobiles: 94 companies, 87 ULTRA HOT** — Unusually high ULTRA HOT rate in autos.
   India's 2025 auto credit cycle created a cluster of INC ratings in Oct 2025.

4. **Energy: 48 ULTRA HOT out of 45 companies** — More ULTRA HOT records than companies
   means some energy companies have multiple instruments escalating. High-priority.

5. **BG + LC account for 37.5% of records** — ACER needs BG rating capability confirmation
   to unlock this pool (3,593 total BG INC in the system).
