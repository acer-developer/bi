## 4 Critical Observations

### 1. CRISIL Still Dominates INC Volume (Confirmed)
CRISIL accounts for **35–45% of all monthly INC records** in every single month. Jul 2025 was the peak: 853 CRISIL INC records in one month. This confirms CRISIL as ACER's primary displacement target.

### 2. BRICKWORK INC Is Declining
BRICKWORK peaked May-Jun 2025 (390-409/month) and has trended DOWN to 153 in Apr 2026. Possible interpretations:
- Companies proactively switching away (good for ACER — they're already shopping)
- BRICKWORK scaling back activity
- Dataset capture effect

### 3. ACUITE + CARE Are Accelerating in 2026
Both ACUITE (258) and CARE (383) hit their **dataset highs in April 2026**. This is the newest wave. If CRISIL is the incumbent to displace and BRICKWORK is the falling player, CARE+ACUITE represent the **emerging vulnerability cohort** for ACER.

### 4. July 2025 Was the Peak Month
**2,018 total INC records in July 2025** — highest single month in the dataset. This aligns with the annual rating cycle (most Indian companies have March fiscal year, ratings 3-4 months after = July). July 2026 will likely see another peak. ACER's calling calendar (1,131 companies hitting 12-month mark in July 2026) is perfectly timed.
