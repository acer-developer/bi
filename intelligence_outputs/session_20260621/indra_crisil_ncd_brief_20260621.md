## NCD Instrument Context

**What is NCD/Non-government debt?**
- Non-Convertible Debentures — corporate bonds issued by companies to raise debt capital
- SEBI-regulated when publicly issued; privately placed when institutional
- Typical deal size: ₹5–500 Cr per instrument
- Rating is MANDATORY for any public/listed NCD issuance
- INC status on NCD = company cannot raise new NCD capital = acute funding pressure

**Why IND-RA NCD INC is ACER's highest-revenue segment:**
- NCD rating fees are typically 2–4x higher than bank credit ratings
- Companies with NCD must keep ratings current for regulatory compliance
- INC on NCD = crisis state; company desperate for new rater
- ACER's SEBI CRA license (once obtained) allows NCD ratings directly
