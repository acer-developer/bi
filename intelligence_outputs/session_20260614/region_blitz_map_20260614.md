## Executive Summary

The July 2026 blitz list contains **3,230 companies** with INC ratings expiring
in June–August 2026. This is ACER's most time-sensitive sales list — these mandates are
being decided RIGHT NOW.

**State detection limitation:** Only 116 out of 3,230 companies (3.6%)
can be geolocated from company name analysis alone. Indian company names rarely contain
city references. The remaining 92.4% require MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) registered
office data for accurate state mapping.

**This limitation does NOT reduce the blitz list's value** — the full 3,230 companies are
highly actionable sorted by agency and renewal month.

## Regional Sales Deployment Recommendations

### North Region (61 confirmed companies + large unknown pool)
- **Key states:** Delhi/NCR (7), Uttar Pradesh (4), Haryana (2), Punjab (2)
- **Top rater:** IND-RA (17 companies) — IND-RA is Fitch-owned, institutional-heavy
- **ACER angle:** IND-RA clients who are INC are mid-sized companies frustrated by big-agency bureaucracy
- **Deployment:** 1 field rep for Delhi NCR + phone/virtual for UP
- **July Renewals:** 29 confirmed (large unknowns likely add 300-400 more)

### West Region (56 confirmed companies)
- **Key states:** Gujarat (18), Maharashtra (12), Rajasthan (6)
- **Top rater:** IND-RA in Maharashtra, mixed in Gujarat
- **ACER angle:** Gujarat has dense SME manufacturing cluster (Surat/Ahmedabad/Rajkot) — prime ACER market
- **Deployment:** Ahmedabad field office critical; Mumbai for Maharashtra
- **July Renewals:** 28 confirmed

### South Region (42 confirmed companies)
- **Key states:** Andhra Pradesh (12), Tamil Nadu (10), Telangana (3), Karnataka (5), Kerala (4)
- **Top rater:** CRISIL/Brickwork in AP, CRISIL in TN
- **ACER angle:** Brickwork has 12 confirmed South INC companies in the July blitz — quick wins
- **Deployment:** Hyderabad (covers AP+Telangana) + Chennai (TN+Karnataka)
- **July Renewals:** 19 confirmed

### East Region (36 confirmed companies)
- **Key states:** West Bengal (8), Bihar (2), Odisha (3), Jharkhand (2)
- **Top rater:** CRISIL (11) + IND-RA (11)
- **ACER angle:** Smaller market, less competitive — ACER can establish early presence
- **Deployment:** Kolkata as base for East

### Central Region (34 confirmed companies)
- **Key states:** Madhya Pradesh (13), Chhattisgarh (3)
- **Top rater:** Brickwork + IND-RA
- **ACER angle:** MP has active Brickwork INC companies — Indore/Bhopal proximity useful

## MCA Enrichment — Next Step to Unlock Full Regional Map

To geolocate all 3,230 companies:
1. Extract CIN (Company Identification Number) from MCA for each company
2. CIN contains state code (2-letter prefix: MH=Maharashtra, GJ=Gujarat, etc.)
3. This would instantly geolocate **all** companies with 100% accuracy
4. Source: www.mca.gov.in/mcafoportal/ (free) or Zaubacorp API (paid)

Priority: State mapping would enable ACER to assign leads to field reps by territory,
dramatically improving conversion rates vs. random calling.
