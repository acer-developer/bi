## INC Analysis

- **D365 INC companies in Manufacturing:** 1,438 (by company × agency)
- **Combined INC list:** 1,619 unique companies — saved to `csv/INC_Manufacturing_20260614.csv`

**INC Implication for Manufacturing:**  
Manufacturing companies with INC ratings face:
1. Blocked working capital renewal from banks
2. Inability to issue LC (Letter of Credit) for imports/raw materials
3. Disrupted supply chain financing (vendor payments held up)
4. Risk of account classification as NPA by lenders

These are URGENT operational pain points — not just rating bureaucracy. Manufacturing INC = real business disruption.
