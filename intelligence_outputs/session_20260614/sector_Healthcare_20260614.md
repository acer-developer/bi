## Executive Summary

Healthcare is a **tier-2 priority sector** for ACER with **807 total companies** identified
across both data files. While smaller than Manufacturing (3,572) or Infrastructure (1,246),
healthcare companies have a distinctive profile: **hospital/clinic operators need annual ratings
for working capital credit lines** and are often managed by doctors/trusts with low financial
sophistication — making them MORE open to a newer, accessible rating agency like ACER.

**Key number: 315 healthcare companies (39.0%) are INC** — not cooperating
with their current rater. For a healthcare operator, INC means frozen WC limits = inability to
purchase medicines, pay suppliers, or fund seasonal bed expansion.

## Top Opportunities — Healthcare INC Priority List

Companies rated INC by Infomerics or Brickwork represent ACER's most immediate entry points:

### Why Healthcare INC is Especially Urgent
In healthcare, an INC rating has immediate clinical consequences:
1. **Working capital credit frozen** → inability to purchase medicines/consumables
2. **Term loan disbursements blocked** → hospital expansion projects stall
3. **Letter of credit unavailable** → imported equipment procurement halted
4. **Revenue cycle pressure** → staff salaries under stress

Every day of INC = operational risk for the hospital/clinic. ACER's pitch: *"We can restart your
rating in 2 weeks. Your credit line resumes."*

### ACER Pitch Script (Healthcare)
- *"Your current agency has marked your company as not cooperating — your bank may freeze your WC
  limit at next review. ACER can give you a fresh rating in under 3 weeks with direct analyst access.
  First call today, term sheet by end of week."*
