## Key Findings

1. **BRICKWORK is ULTRA HOT on 3 of 4 instrument families** (BG 86.5%, Term Loan 86.1%, Fund-Based/Other 80.4%) — the only agency this systemically broken across instrument types. Lead with BRICKWORK in every sector blitz.
2. **IND-RA NCD/Bond (49.4% INC, 674/1,363) is the single best NCD displacement target** — but gated by ACER's pending SEBI CRA license (Oct 8 est.). All IND-RA NCD scripts must include the pre-license relationship-building framing.
3. **ACUITE has zero NCD book** (61 records, 0% INC) — do not run NCD displacement scripts against ACUITE; redirect ACUITE NCD-interested leads to IND-RA or CARE instead.
4. **ICRA and IND-RA are COOL on Bank Guarantee** (14.9% and 10.0%) — do not lead with BG displacement on these two agencies; use the relationship/second-rater framing instead.
5. **CRISIL NCD/Bond is COOL (1.2%, 6/506)** despite being the largest agency overall — CRISIL's NCD book is well-maintained; this confirms NCD displacement should concentrate on IND-RA, CARE, and BRICKWORK, not CRISIL.
