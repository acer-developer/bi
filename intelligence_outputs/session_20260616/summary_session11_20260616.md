## Key Findings — Session 11

1. **IT/Software is 3x bigger than we thought** (812 vs 265). Third-largest sector. BG rating is the killer product — IT companies need it for every contract.

2. **Hotels/Tourism expands to 247 companies**. 85% single-agency is the highest dependency of any sector. 5 of 7 ultra-hot targets abandoned by Infomerics.

3. **Automobiles/Auto is a hidden goldmine** — 309 companies, 67.6% INC, CRISIL (49.5%) charges premium fees to mid-tier dealers. ACER's fee advantage is largest in this segment.

4. **Paper/Packaging has the highest INC rate (76.6%)** — small sector (64 companies) but extremely open to any new rater; companies already understand the rating process.

5. **"Other" bucket is 59% still unclassified** — MCA CIN NIC code lookup is the next step to unlock the remaining 8,356 companies. Estimated unlock: additional 3,000–4,000 classifiable companies.

6. **Chemicals/Pharma was massively undercounted**: 139 → 726 companies after reclassification. Full sector brief now justified.
