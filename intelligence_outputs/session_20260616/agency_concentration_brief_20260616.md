## THE KEY INSIGHT: No Monopoly, Many Opportunities

No single agency holds more than 60% share in any sector — the market is fragmented.
But within this fragmentation, **ACER's vulnerability scoring** reveals which sectors have the right combination of:
- High INC rate (companies abandoning current rater)
- High single-agency dependency (low switching friction)
- High urgency rate (overdue ratings = companies actively seeking new raters)

## TOP FINDINGS

### Finding 1: CRISIL Dominates But Isn't Monopolizing — Good for ACER
CRISIL is the top agency in 12 of 14 sectors but holds no sector above 33% share.
This means clients are already using multiple agencies — they're **not locked in**.
ACER's angle: "Join the companies already using ACER alongside CRISIL/CARE"

### Finding 2: Education Sector = Challenger Agency Territory
Infomerics leads Education sector with 41.4% share — more than CRISIL or CARE.
This confirms that education institutions are **open to non-top-4 agencies**.
ACER can position itself as a credible alternative to Infomerics with better bank acceptance.

### Finding 3: Agro/Food + Textiles = Highest Compound Vulnerability
- Agro/Food: INC 58.9% + Single-agency 79.9% → Score 52.0 (highest)
- Textiles: INC 63.7% + Single-agency 74.7% → Score 50.7 (2nd highest)
Both sectors have Brickwork as significant rater with known displacement opportunity.

### Finding 4: Hotels/Tourism = Untapped Opportunity (90.5% Single-Agency)
87 companies, 90.5% are with a single agency — highest single-agency concentration.
INC rate 38.1% and HIGH urgency 11.9%.
ACER has ZERO presence here — pure whitespace.
Hotels/Tourism is small but concentrated: one regional blitz (Mumbai, Goa, Delhi, Rajasthan) could capture 20+ companies.

### Finding 5: BFSI Has Lowest Vulnerability Score (27.1) — Hardest Sector
Banks are regulated by RBI and strongly prefer top-4 agencies (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE, IND-RA).
NBFCs (addressed separately) are more accessible — but the broader BFSI has highest switching friction.
Don't lead with BFSI in cold calls — reserve for warm referrals.

### Finding 6: Construction = CARE's Strongest Sector (21.3% share)
CARE is the #1 agency in Construction — unusual (CRISIL leads most other sectors).
625 HIGH urgency Construction companies (44.5% of 1,402 total) — active market.
ACER pitch against CARE: faster turnaround + analyst accessibility in smaller cities.
