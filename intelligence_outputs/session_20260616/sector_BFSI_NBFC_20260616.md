## Why BFSI NBFCs Are a HIGH-Priority Target

1. **RBI mandate:** NBFCs with asset size ≥ ₹100 crore or public deposits must get credit ratings from SEBI-registered CRAs
2. **Bank lending dependency:** Most NBFCs borrow via rated NCDs, CPs, or term loans from banks — rating lapse = borrowing freeze
3. **INC rate only 15.8%** for NBFC sub-segment (lower than other sectors) — these are functioning businesses that NEED ratings, not distressed companies
4. **50 HIGH urgency NBFCs** = ratings overdue — they MUST call a new CRA soon
5. **Brickwork / Infomerics NBFCs** = direct displacement targets (both smaller agencies under competitive pressure)

## PITCH SCRIPTS BY SUB-SEGMENT

### NBFC Pitch
**Opening line:**
*"Your NBFC's credit rating from [Agency] is showing as 'issuer not cooperating' — this means your bank lenders can see an active flag on your profile. Under RBI guidelines, this can restrict your NCD issuance and term loan renewals. ACER can issue a fresh rating in 15 business days with a dedicated analyst. Would you like a comparison quote?"*

**Key messages for NBFC:**
- RBI compliance: rated instruments required for bank borrowings above ₹5 crore
- ACER is SEBI-registered — fully accredited for NBFC ratings
- 15-day turnaround vs 30-45 days industry standard
- Named analyst (not a call centre) — you can speak to the analyst directly
- Competitive pricing vs ICRA/CARE — especially for sub-₹500 crore NBFCs

### MFI Pitch
**Opening line:**
*"Your MFI relies on SIDBI, NHB, and bank lending — all of which require a current CRA rating. Your rating from [Agency] is showing issues. ACER specializes in social lending instruments and can fast-track your rating renewal."*

**Key messages for MFI:**
- NABARD/SIDBI lending requires accredited CRA rating
- ACER has experience with social lending institutions
- MFI-specific rating criteria — we understand portfolio quality, PAR30, borrower demographics
- Faster turnaround = earlier disbursement cycle

### HFC Pitch
**Opening line:**
*"Housing finance companies need current ratings for NHB refinancing and market borrowings. Your current rating from [Agency] is showing as overdue. ACER can issue a fresh rating that unlocks your NHB refinancing line."*

**Key messages for HFC:**
- NHB refinancing requires current credit rating
- HFC NCDs need SEBI-compliant CRA rating for public issue
- ACER understands mortgage portfolio quality metrics
