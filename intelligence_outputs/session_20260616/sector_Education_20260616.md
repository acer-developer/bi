## The Education Sector Paradox

**Key insight:** Education sector has the **HIGHEST HIGH urgency rate (31.4%)** but only **30% INC rate** — the opposite of most sectors.

This tells a different story:
- Most sectors have high INC because agencies ABANDON the company (dissatisfied relationship)
- Education sector has high urgency because the **companies simply let ratings lapse** — they don't fight with the rater, they just delay renewal
- This means: Education companies are NOT adversarial towards their raters — they're just **cash-strapped or bureaucratically slow**
- **ACER pitch:** Process efficiency + institutional patience, not displacement

**Why ratings lapse in Education:**
1. Trust-based institutions — not always commercially driven
2. Rating renewal budget competes with campus/faculty expenses
3. Governing body approvals (trustees, VCs) slow the renewal process
4. Season-dependent cash flows (June admission season = cash flush, Feb–March = tight)

---

## Why ACER Should Target Education Now (June)

- **June 2026 = admission season** → universities and colleges just collected fee deposits
- This is the ONE time education institutions have surplus cash for operational expenses like rating fees
- A June call converts; a December call gets deferred to "next year"
- **Window: June 16 – July 31** → call all HIGH + MEDIUM urgency education companies now
