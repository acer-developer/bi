## Why Logistics Now

**Three structural tailwinds** make Logistics ACER's prime mid-2026 pitch window:

1. **Post-COVID infrastructure buildout** — DMIC Phase 2, Sagarmala ports, PM Gati Shakti corridors are generating new rating requirements from logistics players seeking institutional finance.

2. **Bank Guarantee surge** — 28.4% of Logistics companies are rated on Bank Guarantee instruments (for contract bids, customs clearance, port operations). This is a cross-sector instrument ACER can act on immediately.

3. **Brickwork collapsing in this sector** — 94% INC rate among Brickwork-rated logistics firms (16 of 17 companies are INC). This is the single most acute displacement opportunity in any sector.

## Key Intelligence Flags

1. **GMR Aviation Private Limited** — rated by Infomerics in November 2022 (1,700+ days overdue). Rating is severely lapsed. This company is large and likely needs credit facilities. Extremely high priority target.

2. **Brickwork's collapse** in this sector (94% INC) is mirroring what we saw in Auto (41%). Logistics companies that chose Brickwork for lower fees are now stuck with INC ratings and no coverage.

3. **Bank Guarantee concentration** (28.4%) — if ACER can confirm BG rating capability, the addressable pool in Logistics alone is 78 companies with immediate instrument need.

4. **Aviation sub-sector** has high-value companies (Celebi, Nayak, Titan-Antony, GMR) — these are infrastructure-grade firms that may be upgradeable to long-term institutional coverage.
