## Sector Intelligence — Who Are These Companies?

The Automobiles/Auto bucket includes:

1. **Auto Dealerships** (largest sub-group): OEM dealers for Maruti, Tata, Hyundai, Mahindra,
   Hero/Bajaj. These companies need ratings primarily for working capital (CC/OD) and
   inventory financing facilities from banks.

2. **Auto Ancillary Manufacturers**: Parts, components, castings, forgings, tyres.
   These need ratings for term loans and bonds to fund capex expansion.

3. **EV Transition Players**: New-age dealerships moving to EV portfolios, charging
   infrastructure companies. This is the fastest-growing sub-segment.

4. **Auto Finance Companies**: Dealer credit platforms and fleet financing NBFCs.

ACER pitch angle varies by sub-segment:
- Dealers: **fee arbitrage** (CRISIL at ₹4-6L vs ACER ₹1.5-2.5L)
- Ancillary: **speed** (ACER can rate in 15 days vs 45 days for big 4)
- EV players: **sector familiarity** (ACER understands EV growth story)
