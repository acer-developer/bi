## Timing Intelligence

**Why July is the Right Month:**
1. **Annual review season** — Banks conduct credit reviews in Q2/Q3. Companies with overdue ratings need fresh credentials before August CC renewals.
2. **July peak in issuances** — Historically the highest month for new rating issuances (9.7% of annual volume — Session 4 analysis). Demand is at peak.
3. **Kharif pre-season** — AgroFood companies (52 ultra-hot) need ratings before kharif crop financing begins (August–September).
4. **Monsoon construction pause** — Construction and Infrastructure companies use the monsoon lull for administrative/compliance work. Ratings renewals happen now.
5. **Export season prep** — Trading/Exports companies (76.5% INC among their HIGH) need ratings before the August–October export surge.
