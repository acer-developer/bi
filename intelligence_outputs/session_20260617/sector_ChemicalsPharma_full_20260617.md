## Strategic Insights

### 1. Infomerics Displacement Is Clearest Here
Infomerics holds 16.8% of Chemicals/Pharma and has a 26% INC rate in this sector.
108 companies with Infomerics as latest rater + 28 already INC = 28 immediate conversions.
Pitch: "Infomerics has marked you INC. ACER provides a fresh, clean rating in 10-15 days
with a dedicated analyst who will pick up your calls."

### 2. CRISIL Fee Arbitrage — Especially for Specialty Chemicals
CRISIL holds 37.9% (289 companies). For SME chemical companies (turnover ₹50-500 Cr),
CRISIL charges ₹3-7 lakh for annual rating. ACER at ₹1.5-3 lakh = 40-60% savings.
With 102 HIGH urgency CRISIL+CARE companies, the fee arbitrage pitch has maximum impact
when the renewal window is open.

### 3. Seasonal Window: August–October
Chemicals/Pharma has two distinct credit cycles:
- **Kharif (June–September):** Agrochemical companies peak — pesticide/fertilizer orders,
  export credit needed, BG/LC for international trade
- **Rabi (October–December):** Fertilizer, specialty chemical companies seek fresh credit

ACER should target agrochemical sub-segment in July–August ahead of the Kharif season peak.

### 4. NCDs Are an Opportunity
15.9% of companies have Non-Government Debt (NCD/bond) as their primary instrument.
SEBI requires a registered credit rating for NCD issuance. ACER's SEBI registration makes
this directly serviceable — and these companies need fresh ratings to access the bond market.
With 45% INC rate, many of these companies cannot currently issue bonds. ACER fixes that.
