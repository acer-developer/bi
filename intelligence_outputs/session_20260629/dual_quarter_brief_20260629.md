## What Are Dual-Quarter Companies?

Companies with INC ratings that will escalate to HOT/ULTRA HOT in BOTH Q1 2027 (Dec–Mar) AND Q2 2027 (Apr–Jun). These companies have:
1. At least one instrument escalating in Q1 2027
2. A separate instrument escalating in Q2 2027
3. In 37 of 43 cases: **different rating agencies in each quarter** — proven openness to multiple raters
