## What This Is

The complete ACER calling calendar from October 2026 through March 2027.
Combined view: October pool (companies escalating Oct–Nov 2026) merged with Q1 2027 pipeline (companies escalating Dec 2026–Mar 2027).

**No deduplication applied** — a company appearing in both pools (e.g., rated by two agencies at different dates) appears in both, as they represent distinct instruments.

## How to Use This File

1. **Sales teams** open `acer_rolling_calendar_oct2026_mar2027_20260628.csv` in Excel
2. **Filter by Calendar_Period** to see their assigned month
3. **Sort by Tier_Rank then Amt_num** to get highest-priority targets first
4. **Filter NCD_Flag = "NCD — SEBI License Required"** to separate SEBI-gated vs immediate opportunities
5. **Use Call Week column** (Oct pool) or Target_Call_Month (Q1 pool) for scheduling
