## Headline Finding

**IND-RA DID spike in April 2026 — 177 unique INC companies — but the pattern is DIFFERENT from CARE/ACUITE.**

| Agency | April 2026 INC Companies | Pattern |
|--------|--------------------------|---------|
| CARE   | 233 | Fiscal year-end spike (Feb–Apr high) |
| IND-RA | 177 | April spike follows Jan 2026 spike (214 records) |
| ACUITE | 128 | April fiscal year-end spike |

IND-RA is not a one-month-spike agency. It shows **two acceleration clusters**:
- **Jan 2026: 47.6% INC rate** (214 INC records)
- **April 2026: 38.8% INC rate** (186 INC records)

This suggests IND-RA's INC additions are happening in two waves — Q3 end (January) and fiscal year-end (April).
